## Description
Starting a company for the first time? Founder Groundworks gives you the playbook.

Take the Founder Readiness Assessment to find out where you stand, then get daily AI-generated lessons tailored to your weak spots — covering problem clarity, customer understanding, product development, traction, execution, team building, and founder identity.

Each lesson takes just 5 minutes. Mark key points as you learn, ask the AI assistant questions mid-lesson, and track your score as you grow from Early Stage to Founder Ready.

What you get:
- 9-question Founder Readiness Assessment with instant scoring
- AI-personalized daily lessons based on your weak areas
- 7 core skill categories built on the Groundworks Method
- In-lesson AI Q&A — ask anything about what you're learning
- Streaks, score bands, and progress tracking to keep you accountable
- Weekly activity charts and learning goals
- Light and dark mode

Built for first-time founders who want to stop guessing and start building with clarity.
